# North Cycles terms and conditions of sale

## 1. Who we are

North Cycles is a store selling bicycles, accessories and cycling clothing, based in Portland, Oregon. We sell in our physical store and through our website. These terms apply to all purchases made on the website. By placing an order, the customer states that they have read and accepted them. We may change them at any time, but each order is governed by the terms in effect on the date it was placed.

## 2. Orders

You do not need to register to buy, although an account lets you see your order history and download your invoices. When the purchase is completed, the customer receives a confirmation email with the order number, which always starts with NC. The contract is formed when we send that email. If a product sells out after the order has been confirmed, we will let the customer know within 24 hours and offer them an equivalent product or a full refund. An order can be canceled at no cost as long as it has not left the warehouse; just write to hello@northcycles.com with the order number.

## 3. Prices

All prices include a 10% sales tax. Shipping costs are shown separately before payment. Prices may change without notice, but the price that applies is the one shown on the website when the order was confirmed. If, because of an obvious error, a product appears at a price far below its real price, we reserve the right to cancel the order and refund the amount paid. Offers are valid until the date indicated or while supplies last.

## 4. Payment methods

We accept credit or debit card, PayPal, Apple Pay and bank transfer. With a bank transfer, the order is not prepared until the payment has been received, and it must arrive within five days; after that period, the order is canceled automatically. On purchases over $300 you can pay in installments, over 3, 6 or 12 months, with interest-free financing subject to approval by the lender. Card payments are processed through a secure payment gateway; North Cycles never stores card details.

## 5. Shipping

We ship to all 50 states, Puerto Rico and Canada. Orders to the contiguous US (the lower 48 states) are delivered within 2-3 business days of leaving the warehouse; to Puerto Rico, within 3-5 business days, and to Hawaii, within 5-7 business days. Shipping within the contiguous US is free on orders over $50; below that amount it costs $4.95. Shipping to Puerto Rico costs $9.95 and to Hawaii, $14.95. Shipments to Canada may be subject to import duties and taxes, which are paid by the customer. Bikes are shipped in a reinforced box and arrive 90% assembled. If the carrier finds nobody at the address, they will try a second time the next day; after that, the package will be held for five days at the nearest carrier facility before being returned to our warehouse.

## 6. Receiving the order

On receiving the package, the customer must check that the box shows no dents or tears. If the box is damaged, they must note it on the carrier's delivery receipt before signing. Damage caused in transit must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with photos of the packaging and the product. After that period, the damage will be handled as a warranty claim.

## 7. Returns and refunds

The customer has 30 calendar days from delivery to return any product without having to give a reason. The product must be unused, with all its tags and in its original packaging. Bikes must not have more than 6 miles on them. For hygiene reasons, returns of bib shorts or technical underwear that have been unsealed are not accepted. Return pickup is free within the contiguous US; from Puerto Rico and Hawaii, the cost of return shipping is paid by the customer. We will refund the amount within 14 days at most of receiving the product, using the same payment method used for the purchase. The original shipping costs are only refunded if the whole order is returned.

## 8. Exchanges

To exchange a product for a different size or color, the customer must request a return of the original product and place a new order. Shipping on the second order is free if it is placed within 30 days of delivery of the first. We do not make direct exchanges in the physical store for products bought on the website, although we are happy to help you choose the right size.

## 9. Warranty

All products have a 2-year warranty from delivery, including e-bike batteries, which must keep at least 70% of their capacity during that period. The warranty covers manufacturing defects, but not the normal wear of parts such as tires, inner tubes, brake pads, chains, cogs or cables, nor damage caused by crashes, impacts, misuse, lack of maintenance or modifications made by unauthorized shops. To make a warranty claim, you need the order number or the invoice. The product will be inspected in our repair shop; if the defect is covered, we will repair or replace it at no cost, and if it is not, we will send an estimate before doing any work.

## 10. Repair shop and tune-ups

Our Portland repair shop services and repairs bikes of any brand. The basic tune-up costs $39 and includes adjusting the gears and brakes, lubricating the drivetrain and checking the tire pressure and the bolt torque. The first tune-up for bikes bought at North Cycles is free if it is done within six months of purchase. Repairs are estimated before work starts and usually take three to five business days.

## 11. Personal data

We use the customer's data only to manage their orders and, if they have opted in, to send them marketing information. We do not share data with third parties, except with the shipping companies and payment providers to the extent needed to complete the purchase. Customers can access their data, correct it or ask for it to be deleted by writing to privacy@northcycles.com.

## 12. Customer service and complaints

You can reach us by phone at (503) 555-0100, Monday to Friday from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., and by email at hello@northcycles.com, where we reply within 48 business hours at most. Formal complaints can be made in writing to complaints@northcycles.com, or with the Oregon Department of Justice consumer protection office. These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Oregon.
