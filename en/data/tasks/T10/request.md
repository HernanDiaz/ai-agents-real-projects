# T10 · Shipping to Canada and Alaska

The terms say we ship to Canada and to all 50 states, but the program doesn't know about
Canada or Alaska. Canada: $11.95, delivered in 4-6 business days. Alaska: $12.95, in 3-5
business days. Neither of them gets free shipping. Canadian postal codes look like
M5V 2T6, and Alaska ZIP codes start with 995 to 999.
